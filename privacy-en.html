HeePic Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: November 23, 2026

Hebi HeeBox Zhichuang Information Technology Co., Ltd. (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how HeePic (also called HeePic private album / vault) and its iOS, Android and other official versions process information when providing a local vault, media management, private browsing, local-network transfer, backup, feedback and related features.

This policy also covers the website intended at heebox.site/heepic/ and international builds that use the HeePic name. Platforms, regions and packages may offer only some features; platform-specific processing applies only to that platform. Reading this policy does not grant every permission. Processing that requires consent under applicable law is performed only after that consent is obtained.

Key point: Photos, videos, audio, files, links, notes and contacts you keep in the vault are mainly stored on your device. We do not provide automatic server sync of vault content. Passcode protection, file encryption and backup encryption are different measures and may vary by platform. Network configuration, feedback, advertising and diagnostics may still process necessary network or device information, as described below.

1. Information we process

1.1 Local vault content you choose to save

When you import or create photos, videos, audio, other files, links, notes, contacts and folders, the app stores the content, names, groups, indexes and necessary media metadata on the device. Selected items may include sensitive personal information; import only materials you are authorized to handle.

System pickers usually grant access only to the files, photos or locations you select. Export, sharing and backup are initiated by you. Ordinary export may be unencrypted; encrypted backups use a separate password or follow in-app instructions. System file providers, cloud drives or share destinations you choose process exported content under their own policies.

1.2 Passcode, biometrics and security state

The app processes passcode verification, failure counts, guest passcode, security options and required key material on device. Biometrics are verified by iOS or Android; we do not receive raw fingerprints, face templates or your device screen-lock credential. Optional features such as privacy mask, face-down lock, location lock or keep-screen-on depend on device capability, system permissions and app version. We do not receive your app passcode, backup password or decryption keys and cannot recover them.

1.3 Photos, camera, microphone, contacts, location and local network

Permissions for photos, camera, microphone, contacts, location or local network are requested only when you use the related feature—for example importing media, capturing or recording, importing a contact, enabling location lock, or transferring files on the same LAN. Declining a permission does not affect unrelated features. You may revoke permissions in system settings at any time.

Photos access is used to select, import or save photos and videos. The camera is used for intentional capture and, if you enable it, intruder selfies after failed unlock attempts; those photos stay on device, can be reviewed or deleted in Settings, and are not used for facial identification or upload. The microphone is used for intentional audio recording. Contacts access imports the selected contact’s name, phone numbers and email addresses.

Location lock uses location permission, after you enable it, only to decide on this device whether the current place is one of your trusted locations for unlock. Location data for this feature is not used to map your travel history or uploaded to our servers.

Computer transfer starts a temporary local receiver only while you open a transfer page. Files do not pass through our public servers. Local HTTP does not encrypt the transfer; use it only on a trusted network and close it promptly.

1.4 Private browser and clipboard

When you use the in-app private browser, the destination site receives a normal visit request under its own policy. Browser history, bookmarks and related settings stay on device and can be cleared in the app. Some versions may read a URL from the clipboard to suggest adding or opening it; that content is processed on device.

1.5 Website configuration, feedback and diagnostics

The app may request configuration from heebox.site with platform, package name, app version, build and feature switches. The server may receive IP address, request time, protocol logs and request metadata needed for the connection. This request does not include vault content, passcodes or album files.

When you submit feedback, we process the email address and message you enter, category, and diagnostic details such as platform, package, app version, OS version, device model and locale, plus necessary request logs, to reply, troubleshoot and prevent abuse. Do not include passcodes, backup passwords or unnecessary personal content.

Some builds may use crash diagnostics (for example Tencent Bugly, or Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics on international builds) for stability and usage analysis. Those services may process crash stacks, diagnostic logs, device model, OS and app version, network information and an app-instance identifier.

1.6 Advertising and third-party services

China builds may integrate Pangle China (穿山甲). International builds may integrate Pangle Global. Ad services may process device model, OS and app version, network state, IP address, advertising identifiers (such as IDFA when allowed), and ad impression, click and interaction data for serving ads, frequency capping, anti-fraud and measurement. They do not receive vault content or passcodes because of the integration. IDFA / tracking on iOS follows system authorization; you can change it under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

  • Pangle China (穿山甲): Privacy policy.
  • Pangle Global: Privacy policy.
  • Tencent Bugly: Terms / privacy.
  • Google Firebase: Privacy information.

Links you open, system sharing, app stores and file providers process information under their own policies. Visiting the website generates server logs such as IP, time and request path. This privacy page does not load ads, analytics scripts or external fonts.

2. Retention, sharing and security

Local content remains under your control on the device until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall. We do not provide automatic server backup. System backup, device migration or a cloud provider you choose may retain app data under their settings. Uninstalling, clearing data, losing device keys or forgetting the passcode may make content unrecoverable. Keep and verify a valid backup before replacing a device or deleting the app.

Feedback, necessary server logs, crash diagnostics and advertising data are retained only as long as needed for the stated purposes, security, disputes or legal obligations. Except for third-party services you choose, additional consent or legal requirements, we do not sell personal information or provide vault content to advertisers.

We use platform sandboxing, access controls, file or backup encryption and system key protection as applicable, and HTTPS for our public endpoints. Local HTTP and third-party connections follow the feature description. No system is absolutely secure. Keep your device, passcodes, backup passwords and transfer links safe. If a personal-information security incident occurs, we will take remedial measures and fulfill notice and reporting duties required by law.

3. Your choices and rights

  • You may choose whether to use the private browser, local transfer, encrypted backup, feedback, ad personalization and other optional features.
  • You may revoke photos, camera, microphone, contacts, location, local network and tracking permissions used by this build in system settings. Features that depend on a revoked permission may stop.
  • You may view, edit, export or delete local content in the app. Confirm you have originals or backups before deleting local data.
  • Email heebox2025@heebox.site to request access, correction or deletion of feedback you submitted, ask about personal-information processing or withdraw consent that can be withdrawn. We may require reasonable identity verification.

This version usually does not require an online account, so there is no online account deletion flow. For content that exists only on your device, we cannot remotely decrypt, read, delete or recover passcodes.

We usually reply within fifteen business days after verifying a request. If more time is needed or a request cannot be fulfilled under law, we will explain why. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing lawfully completed before withdrawal.

4. Children

Minors should use HeePic with guidance from a parent or guardian. Where required by law, a guardian should read and agree to this policy and help manage permissions, passcodes and backups. Guardians may contact us at the email above.

5. Changes

If purposes, information types, third-party services or applicable platforms change materially, we will update this policy and, where needed, notify you in the app, on the website or by other appropriate means and request renewed consent. The updated policy will be published on this page with a new date.
