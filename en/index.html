Set the privacy boundary first.

Vault content is mainly stored on your device. Opening, importing, backup and transfer are all initiated by you. We do not automatically upload album content to our public servers.

Passcode & biometrics

Set a passcode on first launch, with Face ID / fingerprint. A guest passcode can protect the main vault from casual viewers.

Face-down lock

Place the phone face down to lock automatically when you briefly hand it over.

Location lock

Allow unlock only at trusted places; deny access outside your safe zones.

Intruder selfie

Optionally capture a photo after failed unlock attempts. Photos stay on device and can be reviewed or disabled anytime.

The point is not “upload everything to the cloud.” It is to keep private content in a vault you control, with layered locks at the door.
